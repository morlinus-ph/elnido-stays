El Nido Collection: logo round 1

Nine directions for the shape of the mark. Each is shown on light, on dark, in one colour, next to the name, and as a browser icon at 32 and 16 pixels.

This round is about shape only, so everything is black and cream. Colours come in Phase 2, and the typeface in the name is a stand-in until Phase 4.

What the name holds: El Nido means "the nest". The town is named after the swiftlet nests in its limestone cliffs. Collection means separate stays that share one design language.
