References, round 2

Eight new sites, picked for more character and craft: design-led places in Bali, Sumba, Mozambique, Mexico and Palawan, plus one hotel collection that says exactly what El Nido Collection is about. Each shows the first screen and the screen after scrolling, so you can see how they go from the entrance to their villas or rooms.

Click a name to open the real site. Pick what makes you say "wow", even if it's just one element. Your own reference, The Hideout Bali, blocks automated screenshots, so it's not shown here, but it's noted: intro, then an overview of all listings.

Zannier Hotels

Collection of 6 hotels, Europe, Africa, Asia · www.zannierhotels.com

"Individually exceptional. Collectively unique." That is your concept in one line: separate places, one family of design.

Take
The collection idea right after the entrance, elegant italic serif, each property with its own photo world, calm cream background.
Leave
Video-heavy first screen, small grey navigation text.

Cap Karoso

Beach resort and farm, Sumba, Indonesia · capkaroso.com/en

The most "designed" of all: cream page, huge spaced-out wordmark, photos arranged like a magazine collage.

Take
Editorial photo collage (great for your listing-photo overview), big confident typography, cream + jungle green, lots of air.
Leave
The very wide wordmark style (doesn't suit the El Nido name), slow animations.

Bambu Indah

Artisanal bamboo hotel, Ubud, Bali · www.bambuindah.com

Handcrafted bamboo architecture, like your bamboo ceilings, shown with pride. Logo centred, photo framed with a margin.

Take
Photos inside a frame instead of edge to edge, centred logo with a short tagline, warm off-white, a strong craft story.
Leave
The "We break rules" slogan tone, crowded menus further down.

Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort

Eco luxury resort, Bali · ulamanbali.com

Sculptural architecture and a split layout: photo on one side, text on the other (the side layout you mentioned).

Take
Side-by-side photo and text sections, Google and Tripadvisor rating shown in the top corner, warm cream and gold.
Leave
Gold text that is hard to read, chat bubble, notice bar.

Kisawa Sanctuary

Beach sanctuary, Mozambique · kisawasanctuary.com

Handmade, earthy, a round "Book now" badge. Feels personal and crafted, not corporate.

Take
Hand-built architecture as the hero, a small round booking badge, short warm mission text.
Leave
Seasonal promo as the second section.

NIHI Sumba

Island resort, Sumba · nihi.com

"Edge of wildness": one strong line over a full-screen film.

Take
One short, confident headline; the place does the selling.
Leave
Video only, cookie box over the headline.

Hotel Esencia

Beach hotel, Tulum, Mexico · hotelesencia.com

Completely different: an illustrated hand-drawn house, old-school typography, a full menu written out. Bold and memorable.

Take
The idea of a hand-drawn illustration of your buildings with their arches (unique to you, nobody in El Nido does it).
Leave
The retro blue typography and the long text menu.

Amanpulo

Private island resort, Palawan, Philippines · www.aman.com/resorts/amanpulo

The benchmark for Palawan luxury: very quiet, lots of white space, small serif, photo strip.

Take
Restraint, a horizontal photo strip, sub-navigation for each part of the stay.
Leave
Corporate Aman template; too cold for a personal family-hosted brand.
