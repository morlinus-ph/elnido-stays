## 2. Who ranks on Google

**"el nido villa"**: a Google Hotels block with prices sits at the top, then Villa Finder, Tripadvisor, Expedia, Vrbo, Facebook pages. Only one independent property site appears in the top 20: diwatuvillas.com (position 19). Related searches: "el nido villa price", "el nido villa rentals", "el nido villa reviews".

**"where to stay in el nido"**: Google Hotels block, then blog guides (elnidoinsights.com, mel365.com), Reddit r/Palawan, Tripadvisor forums, Facebook groups, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok reels. No booking portal in organic positions 1–3.

People also ask:
- How many nights should I stay in El Nido?
- Which is better, Lio Beach or Nacpan Beach?
- What is the best month to visit El Nido?
- What part of El Nido is best to stay?
- How many days in Palawan is enough?

What it means:
- For "villa" and "apartment" searches, portals win. A new site won't beat Expedia there soon. The realistic route to those guests is the Google Hotels / vacation rental block (via Airbnb and, later, a PMS with a Google connection) and the stay pages ranking for long-tail terms.
- For "where to stay" and trip-planning questions, honest local guides win. That's exactly where an owner who lives in El Nido can be better than a portal. **The blog is the main SEO lever**, not the homepage.
- Short videos (Instagram, TikTok) show up for "where to stay". The new shared Instagram feeds Google directly.

## 3. What ChatGPT answers

**General question** ("stylish, private place with a view, not a big resort"): House Formo, Perch, Atalaya, Karuna, Lihim, Namal, The Funny Lion, Pangulasian (El Nido Resorts). All have their own websites; ChatGPT quotes those sites and a "best boutique hotels in Palawan" guide (hotelierschoice.com). **None of your stays appear.**

**Specific question** ("private villa or design loft in El Nido town"): **Casa Maligaya is named first**, from the Airbnb listing: "4-story private villa ... rooftop views of the Taraw Cliffs ... home cinema ... hosted by Jessa, a Superhost". Then Pandan Villas, Bahala Na, Lugadia, Panorama, Frangipani.

**Brand question** ("What do you know about The El Nido Penthouse and Casa Maligaya?"):
- Penthouse: correct and positive, from the Airbnb listing (4.94, jacuzzi, Taraw Cliffs view, 120" cinema, Starlink). Says "hosting for about four years" (outdated: it's five) and "247 reviews" (now 251).
- **Casa Maligaya: wrong.** ChatGPT mixes it up with "Casa Malaya", a different resort near Nacpan Beach that is listed for sale, quoting ₱5,000–6,000 prices and DOT accreditation. Guests asking about Casa Maligaya get another property's facts.

What it means:
- ChatGPT only knows your stays through Airbnb. With no website of their own, AI tools fill gaps with whatever looks similar.
- A site with clear facts per stay (name, area, size, what's special, who hosts) plus structured data is the fix. The Casa Maligaya site should do the same.
- "In town" + "design" is a niche where you already show up. Lean into it.

## 5. GEO measures

1. **One fact sheet, everywhere.** For each stay: the exact name, property, area (El Nido town), bedrooms, standout features, rating. Identical on this site, the property sites, Airbnb, Google Business profiles and Instagram bios.
2. **Fact sentences** AI can quote, e.g. "Casa Maligaya is a private two-bedroom villa in El Nido town, Palawan, designed and built by its owners." One per stay, near the top of each stay page.
3. **Structured data**: Organization for El Nido Collection; LodgingBusiness / VacationRental per stay with address area, rating, and a sameAs link to the Airbnb listing and Instagram.
4. **Fix the Casa Maligaya mix-up**: the stay page states plainly where Casa Maligaya is (El Nido town), what it is and who hosts it, so correct facts outweigh the wrong ones. The Casa Maligaya website does the same.
5. **FAQ with real questions** from "People also ask" and guest reviews (rain, power outages and backup, stairs, distance to the pier).
6. **llms.txt** with the fact sheet.
7. **Get listed in the guides ChatGPT quotes**: hotelierschoice.com Palawan boutique guide, elnidoinsights.com, mel365.com. Phase 8 work.
8. **Google Business profile** per property, with the same facts.
