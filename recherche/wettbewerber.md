# Competitors (Phase 2, 2026-10-28)

## Top 3 on Google: why they rank

For "el nido villa" and "where to stay in el nido", Google's organic top 10 is portals (Villa Finder, Tripadvisor, Expedia, Vrbo, Hotels.com), forums (Reddit, Tripadvisor, Facebook groups) and travel blogs. Only one independent property website reaches the top 20 (Diwatu Villas, #19 for "el nido villa"). There are no three real competitors in the top 10 to copy.

So the analysis looks at two groups:
- **Independent El Nido property sites** that ChatGPT recommends or that rank at all. These are the direct competitors for the guest's decision.
- **The guides that rank for "where to stay"** (elnidoinsights.com, mel365.com). These show what content Google rewards.

### Guides that rank for "where to stay in el nido"

- mel365.com: "Where to stay in El Nido > EASY table to compare the areas". Splits El Nido into 4 areas (town, Corong-Corong, Las Cabanas, Lio) with a comparison table.
- elnidoinsights.com: "El Nido's best resorts", updated June 2026, a long list with photos.
- Reddit and Tripadvisor forum threads: real people asking which area to choose.

What they have in common: an **area comparison**, a clear recommendation per traveller type, photos, and recent update dates.

### Common ground (what Google expects)

- Name the area clearly (town, Corong-Corong, Lio, Nacpan) and what it's good for.
- Show guest reviews and ratings.
- Show the stays with photos and a short list of what's included.
- A clear booking path on every page.

### Gaps (where we can be better)

1. **Nobody explains why to stay in town.** Every competitor is outside town (Lio, Corong-Corong, hills, islands). All 5 of your stays are in town. "Walk to dinner, the pier and the cliffs" is an open position.
2. **Nobody shows the owners' design hand across several homes.** Namal has several properties but no shared design story.
3. **Thin pages.** Most competitors have 200–600 words and no FAQ. Real answers to "People also ask" (nights, best month, rain, power) would beat them.
4. **No useful local guide** on any property site. A blog written by owners who live in El Nido fills this.
5. **Proof**: 611 reviews at 4.96 is stronger than any competitor shows on its own site.

### Structure for Phase 3

- Homepage: around 700–1,000 words. Main keyword in title, H1 and first H2.
- Sections: intro / the stays (5 cards, grouped by property) / why El Nido town / designed and built by us / hosted by us / guest reviews / FAQ / guide teaser / booking.
- Each stay gets its own page (300–600 words), with a fact sentence at the top.

Patterns of the strong ones:
1. One clear promise in the first screen (Atalaya, House Formo).
2. Real guest words, many of them (Pandan).
3. The people behind the place, named and shown (Pandan's team). You can't show names or photos, but you can describe the family hosting in concrete detail.
4. A reason to book direct (Atalaya: "commission-free"). For you: later, with the PMS.

Mistakes of the weak ones:
1. No H1, or an H1 like "About Us" (Perch, Lugadia, Diwatu).
2. Superlatives without proof ("best and most luxurious villas in El Nido").
3. Umbrella page with no content (Namal).
4. Cookie banners and pop-ups covering the first screen.
