# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, built as a static site and hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Listings and blog posts are content files, so a new stay or article doesn't mean hand-editing pages. Domain: elnidocollection.com (Cloudflare Registrar).

## Users

1. **Guests of one property.** They arrive from the Casa Maligaya or Penthouse / Cliff Tower websites, usually because their dates are taken there or they want to compare. They already trust the owners and need a fast way to see the other stays and get to booking.
2. **Social and collaboration traffic.** They come from Instagram (@elnidocollection, the property accounts), influencer collaborations and campaigns. They have seen the spaces in photos and want to browse all stays and book.
3. **Google and AI searchers.** They search "where to stay in El Nido", "El Nido villa", "El Nido apartment" or ask ChatGPT, and have never heard of the brand. They need reasons to trust before they click through to book.

Not a priority audience now: property owners looking for a manager. The site must leave room for them later.

## Product Purpose

El Nido Collection is the umbrella website for all stays the owners offer in El Nido. Right now it presents 5 listings on 2 properties and sends guests to each listing on Airbnb. Later, once a property management system (PMS) is in place, it takes direct inquiries and bookings, so the business depends less on Airbnb.

Success means:
- More organic traffic from Google and AI search, helped by a blog.
- Guests who can't get their dates at one property book another stay from the collection instead of leaving.
- Social campaigns and collaborations have one place to send people.
- Later: a growing share of direct bookings.

## Positioning

The owners design and build every space themselves, from start to finish, and host in person with their family before and during each stay. All stays share one recognisable design language that guests come to love, and each stay offers something different. The stays sit at the elevated, luxe end of the El Nido short-term rental market, but feel personal rather than corporate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Now: an overview of all stays, a page per stay, links out to Airbnb, a blog.
- Later: availability search across stays, direct booking or inquiry through a PMS.
- The structure must scale from 5 listings to more properties without a redesign.
- No "Airbnb" in the brand name or domain (Airbnb trademark). Linking to Airbnb listings is fine.
- Undecided: the new name of the Cliff Tower property.

## Product Principles

1. **One collection, many doors.** Every stay page leads to the rest of the collection, so a guest who can't book one stay finds another.
2. **Show the craft.** The owner-designed spaces and the shared design language are the main reason to choose these stays. Let the spaces carry the page.
3. **Personal, not anonymous.** Make clear that Jessa and her family host in person, without showing their faces.
4. **Earn trust from strangers.** Searchers who arrive cold need real proof (Superhost, ratings, guest words) and useful El Nido knowledge before booking.
5. **Built to grow.** Adding a stay, a property or direct booking should be adding content, not rebuilding the site.
